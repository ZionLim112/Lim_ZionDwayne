# My Three Favorite Movies

Movies have always been something I enjoy watching during my free time. Some movies are entertaining because of their stories, while others are memorable because of their characters, music, or the lessons they teach. Three of my favorites are **The Greatest Showman**, **Pride & Prejudice**, and **Percy Jackson**.

## 1. The Greatest Showman (2017)

### About the Movie

*The Greatest Showman* is a musical movie starring Hugh Jackman as P.T. Barnum. The story follows a man who dreams of creating something extraordinary. He starts a show featuring people with different talents and backgrounds. As his show becomes successful, he also faces problems that make him realize what matters most in his life.

### Why I Like It

I really like this movie because of its songs and the story behind them. The performances are enjoyable to watch, and I like how the characters slowly become more confident in themselves.

What I also like about this movie is that it shows how chasing your dreams can sometimes make you forget about the people who supported you from the beginning.

### My Favorite Characters

- **P.T. Barnum** - I like how determined he is to achieve his dreams, even though he makes mistakes.
- **Phillip Carlyle** - I like how he chooses to follow what he wants instead of what others expect from him.
- **Anne Wheeler** - I admire her confidence and how she handles the way other people treat her.
- **Lettie Lutz** - I like how she learns to accept herself and becomes more confident.

### My Favorite Songs

1. **A Million Dreams**
2. **Rewrite the Stars**
3. **This Is Me**
4. **The Greatest Show**
5. **From Now On**

My favorite song is *A Million Dreams* because I like its message about having dreams and believing that they can become possible.

### What I Learned

> Don't be afraid to be yourself, even when other people see you differently.

For me, this movie teaches us that everyone deserves respect and that success isn't always about money or fame. Sometimes, the people around us are more important than what we achieve.

## 2. Pride & Prejudice (2005)

### About the Movie

*Pride & Prejudice* is a romantic drama based on the novel by Jane Austen. The story focuses on Elizabeth Bennet and Mr. Darcy, two people who initially misunderstand each other because of their personalities and first impressions.

As the story continues, they slowly begin to understand each other and realize that their first impressions were not completely right.

### Why I Like It

I like this movie because of its story, characters, and beautiful setting. I also enjoy the conversations between Elizabeth and Mr. Darcy because their relationship develops naturally throughout the movie.

Another thing I like is how the movie shows that people can change their opinions once they understand someone better. It also reminds me that we shouldn't judge others too quickly.

### My Favorite Characters

- **Elizabeth Bennet** - I like how she speaks her mind and doesn't easily change her beliefs just to please others.
- **Mr. Darcy** - I like how he slowly learns from his mistakes and becomes more open about his feelings.
- **Jane Bennet** - I admire her kindness and how she tries to see the good in other people.
- **Mr. Bingley** - I like his friendly personality and how sincere he is toward Jane.

### My Favorite Scene

One of my favorite scenes is when Mr. Darcy walks toward Elizabeth through the morning mist. I like this scene because it feels like a peaceful ending after all the misunderstandings they experienced.

The scenery and music also make the moment more memorable.

### What I Learned

> First impressions are not always right.

This movie reminds me that we should take time to understand people instead of judging them based on what we first see or hear. It also shows that admitting our mistakes can help us become better people.

## 3. Percy Jackson Movies (2010–2013)

### About the Movies

The *Percy Jackson* movies are fantasy adventure films based on the books written by Rick Riordan. The movies follow Percy Jackson, a teenager who discovers that he is the son of Poseidon, the Greek god of the sea.

Together with his friends, Percy goes on dangerous adventures involving Greek gods, monsters, and magical places.

The two movies are:

1. **Percy Jackson & the Olympians: The Lightning Thief (2010)**
2. **Percy Jackson: Sea of Monsters (2013)**

### Why I Like Them

I like the Percy Jackson movies because I have always found Greek mythology interesting. I enjoy seeing the different gods, monsters, and mythical creatures brought to life.

I also like the adventures Percy goes through with his friends. Even though they face difficult situations, they still find ways to help each other.

The movies are fun to watch because they combine action, adventure, fantasy, and humor.

### My Favorite Characters

- **Percy Jackson** - I like how brave he is and how he tries his best to protect his friends.
- **Annabeth Chase** - I admire her intelligence and how she thinks carefully before making decisions.
- **Grover Underwood** - I like how loyal he is to Percy and how he brings humor to the story.
- **Tyson** - I like how kind and caring he is despite being treated differently by others.

### Things I Like About the Movies

- The Greek gods and mythology.
- The different monsters and creatures.
- The adventures and action scenes.
- The friendship between Percy, Annabeth, and Grover.
- The magical places and interesting challenges.

### What I Learned

> Having good friends can make difficult challenges easier to face.

For me, the Percy Jackson movies show how important friendship, courage, and teamwork are. Percy may have special abilities, but he still needs the help of his friends to overcome the challenges they face.

## Conclusion

Overall, these three movies are some of my favorites because they are entertaining in different ways. *The Greatest Showman* has great music and a meaningful story, *Pride & Prejudice* has memorable characters and a beautiful romance, while *Percy Jackson* is full of adventure and interesting mythology.

I enjoy watching these movies because they each have something that makes them special to me. Even though their stories are different, I think they are all worth watching.
